# WindowShade 1.0.16 · 回到收起窗口这一件事

[产品官网](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/) · [窗口往事](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/history/) · [English website](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/en/)

这一版只保留收起窗口和与它直接相关的看一眼、整理卷帘条。新增缩略图和在 App 里更新；新装时不再预设快捷键。标题栏手势、置顶、带到每张桌面、窗口浏览等功能从这一版起删除，见文末。

> 草稿：还没发布。发布前补上安装说明和发布包清单。

## 中文

### 收起后显示

- 设置项改名为“收起后显示”，三个选项：原标题栏、简化标题栏、缩略图。在“设置 → 卷帘”里选。
- **缩略图（新）。** 收起时，窗口在原处缩成缩略图，透明度可以用滑块调整。指针停在缩略图上可以看一眼，单击它，窗口在原处展开；把缩略图拖到别处，展开时窗口出现在新位置。在菜单栏里选“整理缩略图”，缩略图排到屏幕底部，之后可以放回原位。

### 看一眼

- 卡片上不再加文字说明。拿不到实时画面时，卡片上是收起那一刻的画面。

### 快捷键

- 新装时不预设快捷键。在“设置 → 快捷键”里可以为“收起或展开当前窗口”和“整理卷帘条”录制快捷键，也可以打开“按编号展开”（Control-Command-1 至 9）。
- 从 1.0.15 及更早版本升级的，保留 Control-Command-C、Control-Command-0 和 Control-Command-1 至 9。

### 在 App 里更新（新）

- 默认每天自动检查一次更新，有新版本时提示，不自动安装。在“设置 → 权限与启动”的“更新”一组里可以关掉自动检查，也可以立即检查。

### 欢迎使用 WindowShade

- 第一次打开时，“欢迎使用 WindowShade”窗口引导你在系统设置里允许辅助功能和屏幕录制两项。之后可以在“设置 → 高级”里再次打开。

### 这一版删除的功能

以下功能从这一版起不再提供：

- 标题栏手势：在标题栏上两指滑动、两指张开或捏合、两指轻点两下、滚动鼠标滚轮，以及在卷帘条上往下拉或轻点两下。
- 甩一下标题栏。
- 用力按卷帘条看一眼。
- 用 Control-Command 加方向键排布窗口。
- 带到每张桌面（Control-Command-G）。
- 置顶（Control-Command-P）。
- 窗口浏览：停在 Dock 图标上列出窗口，以及菜单里的“选择窗口…”。
- 动态卷帘效果、随设备倾斜和合盖效果。
- 关掉看一眼后，单击卷帘条弹出的小截图。

从 1.0.15 及更早版本升级后，这些功能原来的快捷键不再起作用。

## English

This version keeps rolling up windows and what goes directly with it: glancing and lining up bars. It adds thumbnails and updates inside the app, and a new installation no longer presets shortcuts. Title-bar gestures, pinning, carrying windows to every desktop, window browsing and a few other features are removed; see the end of these notes.

### Show after rolling up

- The setting is now called “Show after rolling up”, with three choices: original title bar, simplified title bar and thumbnail. Choose in Settings → Roll up.
- **Thumbnails (new).** When a window rolls up, it shrinks into a thumbnail in place; a slider sets its transparency. Rest the pointer on a thumbnail to glance at it, or click it to unroll the window in place; drag the thumbnail somewhere else and the window unrolls there. Choose Line Up Thumbnails in the menu bar to line them up along the bottom of the screen, and put them back later.

### Glance

- The card no longer carries any text. When a live picture isn’t available, it shows the window as it was when it rolled up.

### Shortcuts

- A new installation presets no shortcuts. In Settings → Shortcuts you can record one for “Roll up or unroll the current window” and “Line up the bars”, and turn on “Unroll by number” (Control-Command-1 to 9).
- Installations upgraded from 1.0.15 or earlier keep Control-Command-C, Control-Command-0 and Control-Command-1 to 9.

### Updates inside the app (new)

- WindowShade checks for updates once a day by default and tells you when a new version is available; it doesn’t install it on its own. In Settings → Permissions & Login, the Updates group lets you turn automatic checks off or check right away.

### Welcome to WindowShade

- The first time you open it, the Welcome to WindowShade window walks you through allowing Accessibility and Screen Recording in System Settings. You can open it again in Settings → Advanced.

### Removed in this version

These features are no longer available:

- Title-bar gestures: on a title bar, two-finger swipes, spreading or pinching two fingers, double-tapping with two fingers and scrolling a mouse wheel; on a bar, pulling down or double-tapping.
- Flicking a title bar.
- Pressing firmly on a bar to glance.
- Placing windows with Control-Command and the arrow keys.
- Carrying a window to every desktop (Control-Command-G).
- Pinning (Control-Command-P).
- Window browsing: the window list on Dock icons, and Choose Window… in the menu.
- The dynamic roll-up effect, tilt with device, and the lid effect.
- The small snapshot shown when you click a bar with glance turned off.

After upgrading from 1.0.15 or earlier, the shortcuts for these features no longer do anything.
